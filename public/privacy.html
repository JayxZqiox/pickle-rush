PICKLE RUSH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pickle Rush is a free arcade pickleball game. This page explains, in plain language, what information the game keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Account details: your username and your password. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody can read them, including us.
  • Recovery code: also stored only in scrambled form. Only you see the real code, once.
  • Game progress: level, coins, items, rating, match history, challenges, achievements, your character's look (including the nationality you choose) and your friends list.
  • Reports: if players report a username, we keep who reported it and why, so the owner can review it.

Technical information

  • Your internet (IP) address is used for a short time, in the server's memory, to stop password guessing and spam. It isn't saved to the database.
  • If the game crashes, a short error message and your browser type are sent to the server log so the problem can be fixed.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your settings, controls and guest progress on your own device.

Where it's stored

The game server runs on Render and accounts are stored in an Upstash database. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

Who can see what

Other players can see your username, flag, character, level, rating, record, achievements count and recent match scores. Your password, recovery code and reports are never shown.

Children

If you are under 13 (or under the age your country requires), please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Deleting your account

Contact the owner using the email below and we'll delete your account and its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
