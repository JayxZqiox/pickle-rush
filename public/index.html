Play online

Online rooms need the Pickle Rush server. Start it on a computer, then open its address on every device.

  1. Open the pickle-rush folder in VS Code.
  2. In the terminal run npm install once, then npm start.
  3. Open the LAN address it prints, such as http://192.168.1.20:3000, on phones and PCs on the same Wi-Fi.

Local modes, the AI and your local profile keep working without a server.

How to play

The power meter

Hold Hit (Space) and a meter fills beside your player. The shot uses the meter level at the moment you hit the ball. Inside the green band is a Perfect shot: faster and more accurate. Below it is weaker and shorter; past it, in red, the ball flies long. If you don’t hold Hit at all, you still return a normal shot.

Hold left or right while hitting to aim. Load a shot first with Q slice, E curve, R dink or T spike. Slice, curve and spike cost energy, which fills as you hit.

Level up and unlock

Everyone starts at level 1 with 0 MMR. Ranked games, rallies and online matches give coins and XP. New skills unlock by level: Slice at 2, Dink at 3, Curve at 5, Spike at 7 (smashes are always on). Beat each AI to unlock the next one. Spend coins in the Shop on hats, shirts, shorts, shoes, paddles with Power, Control and Spin, special balls like Fireball and Lightning, new courts, and better 2v2 teammates.

Rules

Serve crosscourt past the kitchen. The return and the third shot must bounce before anyone volleys. No volleys while standing in the green kitchen; it flashes red when that’s a risk. Only the serving side scores, to 11, win by 2. Singles: serve from the right on an even score, left on odd; losing the rally is a side out. Doubles: both partners serve before a side out (the first team starts at 0-0-2), and partners swap courts only after winning a point on serve. Only the player diagonally across may return the serve.
